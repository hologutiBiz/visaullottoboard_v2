About Visual Lotto Board

Our Focus

At Visual Lotto Board, we are committed to making lottery information more accessible, insightful, and technology-driven. Through data analytics, visual tools, research, and digital services, we help users better understand lottery trends, historical results, and forecasting information in a simple and engaging way.

Our Role in the Lotto Ecosystem

Visual Lotto Board is an independent digital platform dedicated to providing lottery information, results publishing, analytics, and technology-enabled information services.

We are not a lottery operator and we do not conduct lottery games. Instead, we are terminal operator and we help users access reliable results, statistical insights, historical data, and forecasting tools designed to support informed decision-making.

Our Mission

To become a trusted digital platform for lottery information, analytics, and technology-enabled services by delivering reliable data, innovative tools, and valuable insights that empower users to make informed decisions.

Our Story

Founded in Lagos, Nigeria, Visual Lotto Board was created to improve how lottery information is accessed and understood. What began as a platform for publishing lottery results has evolved into a growing ecosystem offering analytics, forecasting tools, subscription services, and digital information solutions for users across Nigeria and beyond.

Business Information

Visual Lotto Board is a registered Business Name in Nigeria. We provide lottery information, results publishing, lottery-related data analytics, subscription-based online services, research, streaming, and technology-enabled information services.

We proudly serve users across Nigeria while expanding our digital services to other international markets.
