OnimoLotto Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20th, 2026

This policy explains what personal data OnimoLotto collects, why, and what you can do about it. It follows the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

Who we are

Visual Lotto Board, (“we”) is the controller of the personal data described here. Data protection contact: [name or role], privacy@visuallottoboard.com.

We do not collect your card or bank details. Payments are made on Paystack’s page. We receive the payment reference, amount and status, and the email on the payment.

How we use it and who else handles it

We use your data to provide the chat, results and forecasts; to keep your balance and payments right; to send your payment receipt; to prevent abuse and fraud; and to meet legal duties. Our legal bases are performing our contract with you, our legitimate interest in running a safe service, and legal obligation. We do not sell your data and do not use your chats for advertising.

Companies that process data for us:

  • Google (Firebase): sign-in and results data.
  • Cloudflare: hosting, our database, security and email delivery.
  • Paystack: payment processing.

These providers operate outside Nigeria, so your data is transferred abroad. We use providers that apply their own security and data protection terms. We may also share data when the law or a court requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Chats: until you delete them or your account.
  • Deleted chats: erased from your account at once. A private copy is kept for 15 days, only so we can review abuse, then permanently erased.
  • Payment and credit records: kept for 1 year because we must keep financial records. They hold your user ID and amounts, and no messages. After your account is deleted nothing links them to you by name.
  • Request counters and logs: short-lived, for security.

Email fingerprint after deletion (90 days). When you delete your account we keep a one-way fingerprint (a scrambled value) of your email address for 90 days, then erase it. We use it only to recognise the same address if it signs up again, so deleting and re-registering cannot be used to collect the free monthly credits repeatedly. This is our legitimate interest in preventing abuse. The fingerprint cannot be turned back into your email address, and it is not used for anything else.

Deleting your account: use Settings, Delete account. It erases your chats, their 15-day copies and your login. Remaining credits are lost.

Your rights

Under the NDPA you may ask us to give you a copy of your data; correct it; erase it; limit or object to how we use it; and move it to another service. You can delete your chats and your account yourself in the app. For anything else, write to privacy@visuallottoboard.com. We reply within 48 hours. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Security

Connections are encrypted, sign-in tokens are checked on every request, and each user can only read their own chats and balance. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk we will tell you and the NDPC as the law requires.

Children

OnimoLotto is for people 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Cookies and storage

The app stores your sign-in session in your browser. We do not use advertising cookies in the chat. [Confirm with the web team.] Our separate results websites and apps have their own policies, listed in the policy center.

Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new version here and change the date at the top.
